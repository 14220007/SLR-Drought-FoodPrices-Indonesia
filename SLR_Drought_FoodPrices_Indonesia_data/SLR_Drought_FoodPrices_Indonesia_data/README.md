## Search strings
- Google Scholar: S1 "drought" "food prices" "Indonesia"; S2 "El Nino" "rice prices" "Indonesia"; S3 "climate" "food inflation" "Indonesia"; S4 "drought" "rice prices" "Indonesia"; S5 "rainfall" "food prices" "Indonesia"; S6 "climate" "price volatility" "Indonesia"; S7 "El Nino" "inflation" "Indonesia"; S8 "climate" "farmers' terms of trade" "Indonesia"; S9 "kekeringan" "harga beras"; S10 "el nino" "harga pangan"; S11 "curah hujan" "harga pangan"; S12 "iklim" "inflasi pangan" (2022–2026, first 200 results).
- Crossref: C1 drought food prices Indonesia; C2 El Nino rice price Indonesia; C3 climate food inflation Indonesia; C4 rainfall price volatility Indonesia; C5 climate shock farmers terms of trade Indonesia; C7–C15 = S4–S12 (first 1,000 results).
- Scopus: TITLE-ABS-KEY (("drought" OR "El Nino" OR "El Niño" OR "ENSO" OR "rainfall" OR "dry season" OR "climate shock*" OR "climate variab*" OR "climate anomal*" OR "temperature") AND ("food price*" OR "rice price*" OR "commodity price*" OR "price volatility" OR "food inflation" OR "inflation" OR "terms of trade") AND ("Indonesia*")) AND PUBYEAR > 2021 AND PUBYEAR < 2027.

## Exclusion codes
R1 not Indonesia / no Indonesia-specific results; R2 climate shock not measured or analysed; R3 outcome not price, volatility, inflation or NTP; R4 outside 2022–2026; R5 not a primary empirical study; R8 non-food commodity.
